Root causes

WHY IS IT SO HARD FOR YOUNG PEOPLE
TO GET HIRED IN SOUTH AFRICA?

01

Weak economic growth

GDP grew just 1.1% in 2025 while hundreds of thousands more young people entered the job market — the economy simply isn't creating enough new roles to absorb them.

02

The education-to-work gap

Schools and universities aren't consistently producing graduates with the specific, work-ready skills employers say they need, leaving a persistent mismatch between qualifications and jobs.

03

The experience paradox

Entry-level roles increasingly demand prior experience, shutting first-time job seekers out of the very jobs meant to give them their start.

04

Rising discouragement

Nearly 3.9 million people have stopped actively searching for work altogether, meaning the official unemployment rate likely understates the true scale of joblessness.

Our take

South Africa doesn't have an unwilling workforce — it has an unproven one. Millions of capable young people are stuck behind the same locked door: every entry-level job asks for experience that's impossible to get without a job in the first place. We break that loop by letting young South Africans complete real, paid tasks for real businesses and build a verified track record from day one — no degree or connections required.
