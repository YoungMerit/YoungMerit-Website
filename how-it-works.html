Built for Trust

SAFE FROM START
TO FINISH.

Every transaction on our platform is protected by layers of verification, escrow and dispute resolution.

Escrow Payments

Funds are held securely until work is approved. No payment is released until the business confirms delivery.

Identity Verification

All workers and businesses complete ID verification before transacting. You always know who you are dealing with.

NDA Protection

Sensitive business tasks can be covered by legally binding NDAs. Confidential work stays confidential.

Dispute Resolution

If something goes wrong, our mediation team reviews the work history, messages and deliverables to reach a fair resolution.

Employer Verification

Businesses are verified before posting tasks. Workers always know a real, accountable company is behind every job.
