BEFORE YOU WRITE IN

A few quick answers that might save you a wait.

I have an active dispute. Where do I check its status?⌄
Use the Dispute System page and click "Track Your Case" with your reference number. This gives you a live status update faster than waiting for an email reply.
I need to verify my identity. Is this the right place?⌄
No — head to the Identity Verification page to start that process directly. If verification fails or you have an issue with a document, then contact support@youngmerit.com.
How fast will I actually hear back?⌄
Most messages are answered within 1 business day. Dispute and security matters are prioritised. Press and business inquiries may take up to 2 business days due to lower volume staffing on those teams.
Can I call you instead of emailing?⌄
We are currently an email-first support team to keep a written record for every case, which helps especially with disputes and payment issues. Phone support is on our roadmap as the platform grows.
