Root causes

WHY IS IT SO HARD FOR YOUNG PEOPLE
TO GET HIRED IN FRANCE?

01

A steep credential ladder

France's labour market places heavy weight on formal qualifications and institutional pedigree, meaning the type and prestige of a diploma can matter as much as demonstrated ability.

02

Rigid hiring and firing rules

Strong employment protections make employers cautious about first hires, since letting an underperforming junior go later is costly and slow.

03

An uneven apprenticeship pipeline

Vocational and apprenticeship routes exist but don't carry the same prestige as academic tracks, funnelling most competition through a narrower set of traditional paths.

04

Regional and sectoral gaps

Youth joblessness runs far higher in some regions and among those with no diploma at all, showing the burden of the crisis isn't spread evenly across the country.

Our take

France's system rewards the right paper over proven ability. We give young people a second path: complete real paid work for real businesses, build a verified reputation, and let demonstrated skill — not just a diploma's prestige — open the door to full-time employment.
