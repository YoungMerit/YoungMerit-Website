Root causes

WHY IS IT SO HARD FOR YOUNG PEOPLE
TO GET HIRED IN SPAIN?

01

A split labour market

A long-standing divide between heavily protected permanent contracts and far more precarious temporary ones concentrates insecure, short-term work among the young.

02

A seasonal, tourism-heavy economy

Large parts of Spain's economy — tourism, hospitality, agriculture — are seasonal by nature, generating churn rather than stable, long-term entry-level careers.

03

Sharp regional disparity

Unemployment runs markedly higher in the south, such as Andalusia, than the national average, reflecting deep and persistent regional imbalances.

04

An education-to-market mismatch

Persistent gaps between what schools and universities teach and what employers actually need contribute to longer, harder job searches for young graduates.

Our take

Spain's youth unemployment rate has more than halved since its 2013 peak — but it's still the highest in the EU. We give young Spaniards a way to build continuous proof of work across a fragmented, seasonal job market, turning short-term gigs into a verified reputation employers can trust for permanent roles.
