WHAT WE DO

We are an online labour marketplace that connects young workers ("Workers") with businesses ("Businesses") for short-term and project-based tasks. We provide:

  • A task-listing and application system
  • Identity verification and trust scoring
  • A secure escrow wallet for holding and releasing payments
  • A dispute mediation service

We are a technology intermediary. We are not an employer, employment agency, staffing firm, or party to any agreement between a Worker and a Business. The working relationship, including obligations around tax, insurance, and compliance, exists solely between the Worker and the Business.

06

WALLET & PAYMENTS

How the wallet works

Each user has a Wallet. Businesses load funds via supported payment methods (card, bank transfer, or mobile money where available). Worker payments are received into their Wallet and can be withdrawn to a linked bank account or mobile money account.

Escrow

When a Business assigns a task to a Worker, the task payment is locked in escrow. Funds remain in escrow until:

  • The Business marks the task complete and approves payment, or
  • A dispute is resolved in the Worker's favour, or
  • The task is cancelled and a refund is issued per Section 8

Release

Upon Business approval, funds are split automatically: 90% is released to the Worker instantly and 10% is retained by us as the platform fee (see Section 7).

Withdrawals

Withdrawal processing times and minimum amounts vary by country. We are not responsible for delays caused by third-party payment providers, banks, or mobile money networks.

Currency

Transactions are processed in the local currency of the Business's registered country, where supported. Cross-currency conversion fees may apply and will be disclosed at checkout.

07

PLATFORM COMMISSION

We charge a 10% platform commission on every successfully completed task. This fee is deducted automatically before the Worker receives their portion.

Example: A task valued at R1,000 results in R900 to the Worker and R100 to us.

The commission covers platform infrastructure, payment processing, identity verification, dispute mediation, and customer support. No additional fees are charged to Workers for standard use.

We reserve the right to change the commission rate with 30 days' notice. The new rate applies only to tasks posted after the effective date of the change.

08

DISPUTES & REFUNDS

Raising a dispute

Either party may raise a dispute within 7 days of a task's marked completion date via the Disputes page. Disputes raised after this window may not be eligible for mediation.

Mediation process

Our dispute team reviews evidence submitted by both parties (messages, deliverables, task descriptions). We aim to respond within 3 business days. Our decision determines how escrowed funds are released.

Outcomes

  • Worker wins — escrowed funds released to the Worker
  • Business wins — escrowed funds refunded to the Business wallet
  • Partial resolution — funds split per the mediator's determination

Cancellations

If a Business cancels before work begins, the full escrowed amount is refunded. If cancelled after work has commenced, we may award partial payment to the Worker based on work completed.
