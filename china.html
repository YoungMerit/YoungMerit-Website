Root causes

WHY IS IT SO HARD FOR YOUNG PEOPLE
TO GET HIRED IN CHINA?

01

Record graduate volume

Successive record-breaking cohorts of university graduates — over 12 million in a single year — are entering a job market that isn't growing fast enough to absorb them.

02

Property and manufacturing slowdown

A prolonged downturn in real estate and softer manufacturing and export demand have cooled hiring in sectors that traditionally absorbed young workers.

03

A widening skills mismatch

Many graduates are trained for white-collar urban careers, but the openings that exist increasingly sit in different sectors or demand different, more practical skills.

04

Tighter credit for small employers

Small and medium businesses, historically China's biggest employers of young people, face tighter financing conditions and are hiring more cautiously.

Our take

In a market this competitive, a degree alone no longer opens doors — proof of what you can actually do does. We give young people in China a way to build a verified track record of real completed work, so employers can see capability directly instead of relying on credentials alone in an oversaturated graduate pool.

Sources: National Bureau of Statistics of China (NBS), monthly urban surveyed unemployment data, 2025–2026 · Asia Society Policy Institute · Nippon.com in-depth reporting.
